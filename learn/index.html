WESO, the token

WESO is a memetility token on Terra Classic: meme energy plus real utility, built to help revive LUNC and USTC beyond burn-only tactics.

A bonding curve on LUNC

WESO is bought with LUNC directly from its contract. Price follows a published curve: 1 LUNC at launch, 200 LUNC at 1B WESO, rising to 2,523 LUNC at the 7B design cap. Sells settle through a constant-product pool held by the same contract.

LUNC goes to staking

About 80% of the LUNC from each buy is forwarded to the Validator Network DAO, a 4-member multisig, and staked with Terra Classic validators (LUNCLIVE, Alpine Digital, Luna Classic DAO).

Verifiable on chain

Supply, curve position and DAO stake are read live from the chain on our stats page, with methodology published. Use on-chain supply (about 1.016B WESO as of October 2026), not third-party API totals.
