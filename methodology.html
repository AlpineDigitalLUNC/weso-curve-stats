Price

WESO has no order book and no deep AMM pool. Its market is the token bonding curve in the WESO contract itself (terra13ryrrl…jsld36ms, code 11462). The contract publishes its current marginal price as curve_info.spot_price in micro-LUNC per WESO. That number is the listed price.

  • Why spot, not last trade. On a curve every trade has its own average price, and buys and sells settle differently. Spot is the one price the contract itself reports, and it equals the Curves 2.0 spec at the current curve supply (largest gap over the history window: ).
  • What traders actually pay. Buys pay a 1.99% tax (net factor 0.9801), so the effective buy price is about 2% above spot. Sells are settled through the contract's XYK buffer and lose about 1.5%. Per-trade effective prices are in weso-trades.json (price_lunc = LUNC ÷ WESO).
  • USD. price_usd = price_lunc × LUNC/USD. Live: CoinGecko /simple/price?ids=terra-luna. History: CoinGecko /coins/terra-luna/market_chart (hourly for 90 days, daily for 365 days), interpolated to each timestamp.
  • Why the LUNC price barely moves. The curve price depends only on curve supply, so it changes only when buys and sells don't net out. In the history window it moved in LUNC, while the USD price follows LUNC itself.

Supply

Total supply

token_info.total_supply on the WESO CW20: WESO. It has been constant over the whole history window, because curve buys are paid out of tokens already issued and sells return tokens.

Curve supply

curve_info.supply: . This is the curve's own counter and the input to the price formula. It is not total supply: about 327M WESO sit off the curve, mostly in the Capacitor DAO pool.

Max supply

Reported as null. The minter query returns the contract itself with cap null, so the chain enforces no maximum. The Curves 2.0 design plans 7,000,000,000 WESO over 10 years; it appears as design_max_supply and is not used for FDV.

Not used

The weso.world API reports 10B total and 9.31B burned. Those figures don't match any chain query, so they are left out. See the deep dive, section 5.

Circulating supply

circulating = total_supply − Σ balance(non-circulating wallets). The list contains only wallets that are protocol-controlled or DAO treasuries, each identified in the WESO wasm, the DAO Lite pages or on chain. Unlabelled large holders are counted as circulating, because nothing public shows they are locked.

WalletAddressWhy excludedBalance

Balances are read at every sampled block (CW20 {"balance":{"address":…}} query on the WESO contract), so circulating supply is a time series too. Before the historical state window opens (), it is held at the first verified value and flagged circulating_estimated. Open question: the three unlabelled wallets ranked 2–4 hold about 39% of supply. If they turn out to be team, DAO or Capacitor sub-accounts, they should move to this list.

Volume, market cap and TVL

  • Volume. Every successful tx touching the WESO contract is pulled from the LCD tx index (wasm._contract_address='terra13ryrr…', queried in 20,000-block chunks). Each swap event on the curve counts once: buys at the LUNC paid (including tax), sells at the LUNC received. Swaps on WesoSwap pools that move WESO are included the same way (none in the current window). USD uses the hourly LUNC price at the trade time. 24h volume is a rolling 24 hours.
  • Market cap = circulating × price. FDV = total supply × price, because there is no on-chain max supply.
  • TVL (chain) = liquid LUNC held by the WESO contract (bank balance) + LUNC delegated by the Validator Network DAO (terra17x9tpp…), where about 80% of every buy goes. This is the LUNC that backs the curve.
  • TVL (DefiLlama) is shown as published. DefiLlama's methodology: "" Because it also counts the CWLUNC and CWUSTC wrap vaults and the AMM pools, it runs slightly above the chain figure.

How the history is built

scripts/build-history.mjs rebuilds everything from public endpoints with GET requests only.

  1. Historical state (exact). curve_info, token_info, the four wallet balances, the contract's LUNC and the DAO's delegations are read every 4 hours (hourly for the last 48 hours) using the x-cosmos-block-height header. A response is accepted only if the node echoes the requested height. During testing, publicnode returned cached state for other heights, so historical reads use lcd.terra-classic.hexxagon.io. Window: → now.
  2. Trade replay (exact, validated). Before the state window, curve supply is rebuilt by walking every curve trade backwards from the latest verified state: a buy adds return_amount and a sell removes offer_amount. Price then comes from the verified spec. Over all state samples, the rebuilt supply differs from the chain by at most WESO. Window: → now.
  3. Documented snapshots. Points published by the project, plotted separately and never mixed into the daily series: 22 Mar 2026, 39.71 LUNC per WESO (Luna Classic DAO on Medium).
  4. DefiLlama. Daily protocol TVL since DefiLlama listed WESO DeFi ().

Skipped, and why

Daily price history before is skipped: public Terra Classic nodes prune state and the tx index after 2–4 weeks, and no archive LCD was available. With one, the same script covers the full life of the contract (--lcd, --tx-lcd). WESO has no CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap listing yet, DefiLlama has no price feed for it, and GeckoTerminal doesn't index Terra Classic, so there is no third-party WESO price history to fall back on.
